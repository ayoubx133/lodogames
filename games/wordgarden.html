About the game

Swipe across the letters on the wheel to spell a word. Every correct word drops into the little crossword above. Fill the whole crossword to finish the puzzle and grow a new flower in your garden. Find extra bonus words for coins and use a free hint whenever you are stuck.

  • Simple, friendly words made for young readers
  • Every puzzle can always be solved
  • Watch your garden bloom one flower at a time
  • Earn coins and stars as you play
  • Made for Fire tablets, old and new models
  • Plays fully offline

No ads, no in-app purchases, no accounts and no data collection. Read our privacy policy.
