About the game

Swap crabs, fish, turtles, starfish, jellyfish and clownfish to line up three or more. Bubble blockers float up one row after every move, so pop them with nearby matches before they drift away from your plans, and clear algae patches and fishing nets too. Rockets, bombs, propellers and rainbow orbs help out, and two specials together make a huge splash. Each win earns a star to add coral, a pearl clam, a shell castle and more to the reef.

  • Floating bubble blockers that rise every move
  • Cute sea creature pieces and splashy specials
  • Restore coral reef areas one star at a time
  • Earn coins and stars as you play
  • Made for Fire tablets, old and new models
  • Plays fully offline
