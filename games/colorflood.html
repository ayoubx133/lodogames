About the game

Your flood starts in the top corner of a bright board of candy tiles. Tap a color and the whole flood turns that color, swallowing every touching tile that matches. Plan your colors to cover the whole board within the move limit. Boards grow bigger and more colorful as you go, and a hint button is always ready when you need a nudge.

  • Simple tap to play color flood puzzles
  • Boards grow from tiny to giant
  • Cute flood buddy and six tile styles to unlock
  • Earn coins and stars as you play
  • Made for Fire tablets, old and new models
  • Plays fully offline
