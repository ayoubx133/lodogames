Cone Cupcake Bakery

Coming soon to the Amazon Appstore for Fire tablets

Bake fluffy cupcakes inside crunchy waffle cones for cute animal customers.

About the game

Cute animal customers come to your bakery with an order card. Hold to pour the batter up to the line, bake it in the oven and let go in the golden zone, then circle your finger to swirl the frosting high. Shake rainbow sprinkles, drop choco chips and drag a cherry on top before you serve. Match the order to earn three stars and coins for fancy dipped cones.

  • Pour, bake, swirl and decorate with your finger
  • Cute animal customers with their own orders
  • Six dipped cone styles to collect
  • Earn coins and stars as you play
  • Made for Fire tablets, old and new models
  • Plays fully offline
