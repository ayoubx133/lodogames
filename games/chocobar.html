Choco Bar Factory

CasualFreeOfflineNo ads

Coming soon to the Amazon Appstore for Fire tablets

Melt, pour, freeze and wrap your own chocolate bars in a busy candy factory.

About the game

Read the factory order and run every machine with your finger. Drop chocolate in the copper pot and stir until it melts, steer the pouring machine over each mould and fill it to the line, then sprinkle nuts or candy. Pull the freezer lever, tap the mould to pop the bar out and design a shiny wrapper with stickers before folding it shut. Match the order for three stars and unlock heart, star and bear shaped moulds.

  • Moving machines on a real conveyor belt
  • Melt, pour, mix, freeze and wrap every bar yourself
  • Design wrappers with foil colors and cute stickers
  • Earn coins and stars as you play
  • Made for Fire tablets, old and new models
  • Plays fully offline
