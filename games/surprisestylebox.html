About the game

Every level brings a theme like a birthday party, a rainy day or a royal ball. First shake and tap the mystery gift box until it bursts open with a new outfit or hairstyle for your closet. Then dress the doll from your closet to match the theme wishes and the cute judges score your look. Collect all the looks and earn coins for new rooms.

  • Shake and tap mystery boxes for surprise reveals
  • Collect outfits and hairstyles into your closet
  • Fun themes with cute judges and three star looks
  • Earn coins and stars as you play
  • Made for Fire tablets, old and new models
  • Plays fully offline
