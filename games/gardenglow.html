About the game

Swap tulips, bluebells, sunflowers, violets, roses and marigolds to make rows of three or more. Clear leafy bushes, cut creeping vines and turn dry soil into blooming grass to finish each level goal. Bigger matches make rockets, bombs, propellers and rainbow orbs that you can combine for huge garden blasts. Every win earns a star to place a gazebo, fountain, flower arch and more in a glowing sunset garden.

  • Relaxing flower match 3 with satisfying combos
  • Dry soil blooms into fresh grass as you match on it
  • Restore a magic garden area by area with lovely decorations
  • Earn coins and stars as you play
  • Made for Fire tablets, old and new models
  • Plays fully offline
