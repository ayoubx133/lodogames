Pour colorful sand layers into glass jars to match each calm pattern.

About the game

Hold your finger to pour the sand bottle and slide it to choose where the sand falls. The sand piles up, slides down the slopes and keeps its pretty layers. Follow the dotted line to shape each layer like the pattern card, and when every bottle is empty your jar is compared with the card. Slow, soothing music and soft sand sounds make every jar relaxing.

  • Real piling and sliding sand
  • Match the pattern for three stars
  • Six jar styles to collect
  • Earn coins and stars as you play
  • Made for Fire tablets, old and new models
  • Plays fully offline
