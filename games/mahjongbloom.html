Match fruit and flower tiles and travel to a new happy place each level!

About the game

Tap two matching tiles that are free to remove them from the board. A tile is free when nothing is on top of it and one side is open. Clear every tile to reach the next stop on your trip across a colorful map. Use a hint or mix the tiles any time, every board can always be cleared.

  • Cute fruit and flower tiles that are easy to tell apart
  • Every board is built to be solvable
  • Travel map with a new place to reach each level
  • Earn coins and stars as you play
  • Made for Fire tablets, old and new models
  • Plays fully offline

No ads, no in-app purchases, no accounts and no data collection. Read our privacy policy.
