About the game

Sudoku Pals Picture swaps numbers for cute animal friends like a cat, frog, chick, pig, bunny and panda. Pick a pal and tap an empty square so every row, column and box has exactly one of each. Start with tiny 4 by 4 boards and grow into 6 by 6 puzzles, each with one clear answer. Only the right pal fits, and a hint is always there, so you never get stuck.

  • Picture sudoku with six cute animal pals
  • Easy 4 by 4 boards that grow into 6 by 6
  • Gentle hints, happy animal sounds and board themes to unlock
  • Earn coins and stars as you play
  • Made for Fire tablets, old and new models
  • Plays fully offline
