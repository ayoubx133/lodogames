Swap royal crowns and jewels in match 3 puzzles and rebuild a fairy tale castle.

About the game

Swap two neighbouring pieces to line up three or more crowns, shields, gems, keys, potions or crests. Match four in a row for a rocket, make an L or T shape for a bomb, a square for a spinning propeller and five in a row for a rainbow orb, then swap two specials together for giant combos. Every level has a clear goal like breaking crates, melting frost tiles or freeing chained pieces, and if you run out of moves you can always add five more for free. Each win gives you a star to place a glowing decoration and restore the castle room by room.

  • Juicy match 3 levels with rockets, bombs, propellers and rainbow orbs
  • Rebuild a fairy tale castle with chandeliers, thrones and banners
  • Free extra moves when you get stuck, never a hard wall
  • Earn coins and stars as you play
  • Made for Fire tablets, old and new models
  • Plays fully offline
