Find hidden objects in busy, colorful village scenes with your spy glass.

About the game

Explore a sunny market square and a seaside harbor full of tiny details. Every level hides a list of objects like a teddy, a crown or a kite, tucked on counters, boats and flower carts. Tap an object to find it, drag to look around and zoom in with the plus button or a double tap. Stuck? The spy glass hint shows you where to look.

  • Busy painted village and harbor scenes
  • Zoom, pan and a spy glass hint
  • New object spots and harder hiding every level
  • Earn coins and stars as you play
  • Made for Fire tablets, old and new models
  • Plays fully offline
