About the game

Tap a card to flip it, then tap another to find its matching critter friend. Matching pairs stay open and cheer, while the others flip back so you can remember where they hide. Make matches in a row for combos and finish quickly for a time bonus. Boards grow from three pairs to eighteen, with look-alike critters in different colors for extra brain training.

  • Classic flip and match memory game for all ages
  • Dozens of cute critters with happy animations
  • Combos, time bonus and six card designs to unlock
  • Earn coins and stars as you play
  • Made for Fire tablets, old and new models
  • Plays fully offline
