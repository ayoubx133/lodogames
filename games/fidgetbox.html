Open a box of 12 satisfying fidget toys and finish calm little goals for stars.

About the game

Each level opens a fidget toy from the box: a simple dimple, a spinner, a clicker cube, a slider, a squishy mochi, light switches, bubble wrap, gears, a boing spring, a flip cube, spin rings and a pop tube. Every toy has three relaxing goals, like spinning fast or popping every dimple, and each goal gives a star. Finish the first goal to unlock the done button, or keep playing for all three stars. Every level you finish unlocks the next toy in your box.

  • 12 different fidget toys to unlock
  • Clicky, poppy and springy sounds
  • Calm goals with no timers
  • Earn coins and stars as you play
  • Made for Fire tablets, old and new models
  • Plays fully offline
