Dream Cake Maker DIY

CasualFreeOfflineNo ads

Coming soon to the Amazon Appstore for Fire tablets

Stack, frost, drip and decorate dreamy layer cakes for cute animal friends.

About the game

Every cute animal customer brings an order card for the cake of their dreams. Tap to drop the sponge layers onto the turntable and line them up, then swipe to spin the cake while the spatula smooths the frosting all the way round. Drag along the top edge to pour a glossy drip, then add fresh berries and the right number of candles. Match the order to earn three stars and coins for new cake boards.

  • Stack, spin, frost and drip with your finger
  • Cute animal customers with their own orders
  • Six pretty cake boards to collect
  • Earn coins and stars as you play
  • Made for Fire tablets, old and new models
  • Plays fully offline
