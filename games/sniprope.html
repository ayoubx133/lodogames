About the game

Munch the friendly monster is hungry and the candy is hanging on ropes. Swipe across a rope to snip it and watch the candy swing, fall and fly. Time your snips to collect all three stars on the way and drop the candy right into Munch's mouth. Later levels add floating bubbles, air puffers and hook pins that catch the candy with a new rope.

  • Swing physics puzzles with a cute hungry monster
  • Bubbles, air puffers and hook pins to master
  • Collect three stars per level and unlock six tasty candies
  • Earn coins and stars as you play
  • Made for Fire tablets, old and new models
  • Plays fully offline
