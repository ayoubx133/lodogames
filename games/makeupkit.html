Makeup Kit Studio

Coming soon to the Amazon Appstore for Fire tablets

Make your own lipstick and eyeshadow, then do a cute makeover at the mirror.

About the game

First you make the makeup: scoop pigments into the balm dish and stir until the lipstick shade matches the look card, pour it into the mould and twist the new lipstick up. Pour shimmer powder into the press and pull it down to set your eyeshadow. Then rub the eyeshadow, lipstick and blush onto Mia at the vanity mirror with your finger. Match the look for three stars and unlock new mirror styles.

  • Mix pigments to create your own lipstick shades
  • Press shimmer eyeshadow and twist up real looking lipsticks
  • Paint the makeup on with your finger at a glowing vanity mirror
  • Earn coins and stars as you play
  • Made for Fire tablets, old and new models
  • Plays fully offline
