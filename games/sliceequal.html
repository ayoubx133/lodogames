About the game

Swipe a straight line across the treat to cut it, then see the size of every piece in percent. Share pizza, cake, cheese, watermelon and fun cookies in halves, thirds or quarters with one or two cuts. Later treats come with cherries, so every piece must get exactly one. The closer your pieces are to equal, the more stars you earn.

  • Satisfying swipe to slice puzzles with percent results
  • Halves, thirds and quarters on a dozen tasty treats
  • Six fun knives to unlock, from wooden to laser
  • Earn coins and stars as you play
  • Made for Fire tablets, old and new models
  • Plays fully offline
