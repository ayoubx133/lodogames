About the game

Hold your finger on the screen to grow a wooden stick from the edge of the pillar. Let go and the stick drops down to become a bridge. If it is just the right length the kid walks across, but too short or too long means a splash into the pond. Land the tip on the little red target for a PERFECT bonus and reach the flag to finish the level.

  • One finger control: hold to grow, let go to drop
  • Perfect bonus target on every pillar for extra coins
  • Six cute kids to unlock and sliding pillars in later levels
  • Earn coins and stars as you play
  • Made for Fire tablets, old and new models
  • Plays fully offline
