About the game

Start at the glowing hive cell and swipe one trail that visits every honeycomb cell exactly once. Flowers paint your trail pink, blue or purple, and flower gates only open for a trail of the same colour, so plan your route. Slide back to undo, tap HINT to see the next moves, or RESET to start fresh. The honeycombs grow step by step and every level always has a solution.

  • One trail puzzles on hexagon honeycombs
  • Colour flowers and gates make every route a new riddle
  • Cute bees, a ladybug and a queen bee to unlock
  • Earn coins and stars as you play
  • Made for Fire tablets, old and new models
  • Plays fully offline
