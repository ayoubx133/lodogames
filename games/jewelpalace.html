About the game

Swap two neighbouring jewels to line up three or more hearts, sapphires, emeralds, gold stars, amethysts or diamonds. Melt single and double ice tiles, smash frozen ice blocks and free jewels locked in chains before your moves run out. Make rockets, bombs, propellers and rainbow orbs, and swap two specials together for dazzling combos, with five free moves whenever you need them. Every win earns a star to place a grand clock, velvet sofa, crystal vase, piano and more in the palace.

  • Classic jewel swap levels focused on ice and chains
  • Dazzling specials and combos
  • Furnish an elegant palace room by room
  • Earn coins and stars as you play
  • Made for Fire tablets, old and new models
  • Plays fully offline
