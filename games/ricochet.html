About the game

You have one foam dart per level. Drag to aim and watch the dotted line show how the dart will bounce off walls, shiny mirrors and rubber pegs. Line it up so the dart pops every balloon robot in one throw, then let go and enjoy the chain of pops. Plan your angle, use the bounces and become the trick shot hero.

  • One throw puzzles with a helpful bounce preview
  • Toy block walls, mirrors and rubber pegs to bounce off
  • Six kid heroes and colorful darts to unlock
  • Earn coins and stars as you play
  • Made for Fire tablets, old and new models
  • Plays fully offline
