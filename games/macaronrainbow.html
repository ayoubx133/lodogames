Macaron Rainbow Maker

CasualFreeOfflineNo ads

Coming soon to the Amazon Appstore for Fire tablets

Mix rainbow colors, pipe, bake and fill cute macarons for sweet pet customers.

About the game

Read the order ticket, then squeeze color drops into the batter and stir in circles to mix a rainbow color. Hold the piping bag over each circle to pipe perfect shells, tap the tray to pop the bubbles and stop the oven when the dial is green. Swirl the cream, drag the tops on to make the sandwich and decorate with pearls or a drizzle. Match the order to earn up to three stars and unlock new baking mats.

  • Mix red, yellow and blue to make six rainbow colors
  • Pipe, bake, fill and sandwich every macaron with your finger
  • Cute pet customers in a cozy Paris bakery
  • Earn coins and stars as you play
  • Made for Fire tablets, old and new models
  • Plays fully offline
