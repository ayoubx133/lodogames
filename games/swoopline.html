About the game

Start on the glowing block and swipe one continuous line that fills every block of the shape. You can never visit a block twice, so plan your route around holes and little walls. Slide back over your line to undo, or tap HINT to see the next few moves. Shapes grow bigger and trickier step by step, and every level always has a solution.

  • One finger swipe puzzles that start easy for kids
  • Holes and walls make bigger shapes a fun brain teaser
  • Cute birds and colourful ribbons to unlock
  • Earn coins and stars as you play
  • Made for Fire tablets, old and new models
  • Plays fully offline
