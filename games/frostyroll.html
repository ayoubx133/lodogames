Pour, chop, spread and scrape rolled ice cream for cute animal customers.

About the game

Cute animal customers visit your seaside ice cream stall with an order card. Hold to pour sweet cream onto the frosty steel plate, chop in strawberries, cookies or chocolate, and drag the scraper to spread a thin sheet that freezes. Push the scraper up each strip to curl perfect rolls that hop into the cup, then add choco sauce, strawberries and wafer sticks. Match the order to earn three stars and coins for cute cups.

  • Pour, chop, spread and scrape real rolls
  • Cute animal customers and sunny ukulele music
  • Six colorful cups to collect
  • Earn coins and stars as you play
  • Made for Fire tablets, old and new models
  • Plays fully offline
