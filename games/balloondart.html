About the game

Tap to throw a dart at the spinning carnival wheel. Time your throw so the dart pops a balloon instead of bumping a star block or another dart. Pop every balloon before you run out of darts to finish the level. Golden star balloons give extra darts and gift balloons give coins.

  • Spinning wheels that speed up, slow down and swing
  • Star balloons, gift balloons and tough double balloons
  • Six fun dart styles to unlock
  • Earn coins and stars as you play
  • Made for Fire tablets, old and new models
  • Plays fully offline

No ads, no in-app purchases, no accounts and no data collection. Read our privacy policy.
