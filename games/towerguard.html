Tiny Tower Guard

Coming soon to the Amazon Appstore for Fire tablets

Build toy towers and keep the silly slimes away from the cookie jar!

About the game

Silly slime blobs hop down the garden path to grab the cookies. Tap a wooden pad to build a toy tower: the Popper shoots foam balls, Bubbles slows slimes down and Jelly Pop splashes groups. Earn candy from every slime to build and upgrade more towers. Clear all waves with cookies left in the jar to win.

  • Three toy towers with three upgrade levels each
  • Gentle, cute slimes that hop away happy
  • New winding garden paths every level
  • Earn coins and stars as you play
  • Made for Fire tablets, old and new models
  • Plays fully offline

No ads, no in-app purchases, no accounts and no data collection. Read our privacy policy.
