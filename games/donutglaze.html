Coming soon to the Amazon Appstore for Fire tablets

Fry, flip and glaze yummy donuts for cute animal customers at a retro diner.

About the game

Cute animal customers line up at your retro diner with an order card. Tap to drop the dough into the fryer, flip it when it turns golden and lift it out, then drag it down into a bowl of shiny glaze. Drizzle wavy lines with the bottle, shake rainbow sprinkles and tap on fluffy marshmallows. Match the order to earn three stars and coins for new serving plates.

  • Fry, flip, dip and drizzle with your finger
  • Cute animal customers and diner rock music
  • Six fun serving plates to collect
  • Earn coins and stars as you play
  • Made for Fire tablets, old and new models
  • Plays fully offline
