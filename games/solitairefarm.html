About the game

Tap a face up card that is one higher or one lower than the open card to clear it. Draw a new card from the pile when you have no match. Clear all the cards from the peaks to win the round and earn coins. Spend your coins to build a barn, a pond, a windmill and much more on your farm.

  • Every deal is checked so it can be won
  • Build sixteen farm pieces with your coins
  • Free undo and long streak bonuses
  • Earn coins and stars as you play
  • Made for Fire tablets, old and new models
  • Plays fully offline
