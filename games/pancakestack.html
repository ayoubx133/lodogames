Fluffy Pancake Stack

CasualFreeOfflineNo ads

Coming soon to the Amazon Appstore for Fire tablets

Pour, flip and stack fluffy pancakes with syrup for cute animal customers.

About the game

Cute animal customers come for breakfast with an order card. Hold to pour the batter into the pan up to the ring, wait for the bubbles and swipe up to flip each pancake when it turns golden. Stack them high, drag on the butter, pour syrup that drips down the sides, add fresh berries and a swirl of whipped cream. Match the order to earn three stars and coins for colorful pans.

  • Pour, flip and stack with your finger
  • Cute animal customers and sunny morning music
  • Six colorful frying pans to collect
  • Earn coins and stars as you play
  • Made for Fire tablets, old and new models
  • Plays fully offline
