Match sweet pastries, stop the growing frosting and decorate a bakery street.

About the game

Swap macarons, croissants, donuts, cupcakes, lemon tarts and berry muffins to make matches of three or more. Clear frosting cake blocks before they spread to the next tile, and wipe away pink frosting smears to reach each goal. Make rockets, bombs, propellers and rainbow orbs and mix them together for delicious combos. Each win gives a star to build a parade street with a cake float, bakery cart, cupcake lamps and balloons.

  • Sweet match 3 levels full of pastries and sparkles
  • Frosting blocks grow if you leave them alone, so plan ahead
  • Decorate a cheerful bakery parade street
  • Earn coins and stars as you play
  • Made for Fire tablets, old and new models
  • Plays fully offline
