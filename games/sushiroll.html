Sushi Roll Master

CasualFreeOfflineNo ads

Coming soon to the Amazon Appstore for Fire tablets

Spread rice, add fillings, roll and slice kawaii sushi for hungry pet friends.

About the game

Read the order from your pet customer and lay the nori on the bamboo mat. Rub with your finger to spread the rice, drag salmon, cucumber, avocado, egg or crab onto it, then swipe up to roll the mat. Tap on the marks to slice the roll and put every piece on the board, where it wakes up with a cute face. Clean cuts and the right fillings earn up to three stars.

  • Spread, fill, roll and slice every roll with your finger
  • Kawaii sushi faces: kitty, bunny, bear, panda and frog styles
  • Relaxing Japanese pop music in a sunset sushi bar
  • Earn coins and stars as you play
  • Made for Fire tablets, old and new models
  • Plays fully offline
