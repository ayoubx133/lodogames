About the game

Each chalkboard picture is made of lines: a tent, a fish, a cat, a rocket and many more. Trace every line exactly once without lifting your finger, then the picture fills with colour and comes alive. If you get stuck, tap UNDO or HINT to see where to start. Pictures get bigger step by step and every one of them can always be drawn in one stroke.

  • Over 20 cute chalk pictures that fill with colour
  • Starts easy for kids, grows into a real brain teaser
  • Collect colourful chalk sticks with your coins
  • Earn coins and stars as you play
  • Made for Fire tablets, old and new models
  • Plays fully offline
