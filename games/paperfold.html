About the game

Every level gives you a colorful paper sheet with dashed creases and cute paper pals printed on both sides. Tap a crease tab to fold that flap over and watch the paper flip with a satisfying fold. Pick the right creases in the right order so your sheet matches the goal picture. Undo, reset and a friendly hint are always there, and the sheets grow bigger as you go.

  • Satisfying paper folding logic puzzles
  • Cute paper pals hiding on both sides of the sheet
  • Six paper patterns to unlock, from dots to gold foil
  • Earn coins and stars as you play
  • Made for Fire tablets, old and new models
  • Plays fully offline
