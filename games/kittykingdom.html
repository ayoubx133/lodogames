Match playful cat toys, burst yarn bombs and build a royal cat palace.

About the game

Swap yarn balls, toy fish, felt mice, golden bells, feather wands and paw cushions to line up three or more. Make an L or T shape for a giggling yarn bomb that bursts into flying threads, plus rockets, propellers and rainbow orbs, and swap two specials together for huge combos. Open cardboard boxes, clear fluffy rug tiles, untangle yarn knots and bring sleepy kittens down to the bottom, with free extra moves whenever you need them. Each win earns a star to add a cat tree, royal bed, kitty castle and more to the palace.

  • Playful yarn bomb specials and big combos
  • Sleepy kittens, cardboard boxes and yarn knot goals
  • Decorate a royal cat palace one star at a time
  • Earn coins and stars as you play
  • Made for Fire tablets, old and new models
  • Plays fully offline
