About the game

Slice soft blocks of colorful kinetic sand with a swipe of your finger. Follow the dashed guide line and the cut piece tips over and crumbles into a pour of sand that piles up in the glass jar. Every level is a new order of slices, layers, corners and tilted cuts on fun sand shapes. Cut close to the line to earn three stars, and relax to calm lo-fi music.

  • Satisfying slicing with crumbling sand and soft sounds
  • New orders of slices, layers, corners and tilted cuts
  • Six fun knives to unlock including rainbow and crystal
  • Earn coins and stars as you play
  • Made for Fire tablets, old and new models
  • Plays fully offline
