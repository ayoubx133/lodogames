Slidey Picture Fix

PuzzleFreeOfflineNo ads

Coming soon to the Amazon Appstore for Fire tablets

Slide the tiles to fix cute pictures in a calm sliding block puzzle.

About the game

Each picture is mixed up into sliding tiles with one empty space. Tap a tile next to the gap, or a whole row at once, to slide it, and put the picture back together. Hold PEEK to see the finished picture and tap HINT when you get stuck. Boards grow from 3 by 3 to 5 by 5, and fewer moves earn more stars and coins for new tile frames.

  • Six cute pictures from kittens to castles
  • Boards from 3 by 3 up to 5 by 5, always solvable
  • Peek, hint and number helpers for every age
  • Earn coins and stars as you play
  • Made for Fire tablets, old and new models
  • Plays fully offline

No ads, no in-app purchases, no accounts and no data collection. Read our privacy policy.
