Slime Lab Mix

Coming soon to the Amazon Appstore for Fire tablets

Mix glue, colors, glitter and charms into slime, then stretch and squish it.

About the game

Read the order card, then make the slime yourself: hold to pour the glue, tap color drops in, shake glitter over the bowl and stir in circles until it turns into slime. Drop in cute charms, then poke, squish and stretch your soft and wobbly slime with your finger. Match the order to earn three stars. Unlock new slime types like cloud, jelly, butter, crunchy and galaxy.

  • Mix colors that really blend
  • Squishy stretchy slime physics
  • Six slime types to unlock
  • Earn coins and stars as you play
  • Made for Fire tablets, old and new models
  • Plays fully offline
