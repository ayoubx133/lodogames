About the game

Guide a kid spy through the museum vault to reach the treasure at the end of the hall. Hold your finger down to sneak forward and let go to stop and wait. Watch the blinking laser gates, patrol drones, spinning beams and security cameras, then slip through at just the right moment. Get spotted and the silly alarm rings, so try again and find the perfect timing.

  • Simple hold and release control with clever timing puzzles
  • Laser gates, patrol drones, spinning beams and cameras
  • Six kid spies to unlock with coins you earn
  • Earn coins and stars as you play
  • Made for Fire tablets, old and new models
  • Plays fully offline
