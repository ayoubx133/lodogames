About the game

Each picture is missing something: the cup needs a handle, the car needs a wheel, the cat needs a tail. Look closely, then draw the missing bit with your finger right where it belongs. The smart checker is very forgiving, so wobbly lines from little hands count too. When you get it right, the picture comes alive with a funny surprise, and HINT shows a dotted guide whenever you need help.

  • Over 20 funny pictures to finish with your finger
  • Kind shape checker made for kids, no fail screens
  • Fun pencils, crayons and markers to unlock
  • Earn coins and stars as you play
  • Made for Fire tablets, old and new models
  • Plays fully offline
