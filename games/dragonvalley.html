About the game

Swap fire, ice, leaf, sun, star and heart crystals to make matches of three or more. Break dragon eggs with two hits: the first cracks the shell and the second lets a tiny dragon flutter free. Clear crystal floor tiles and chains, and combine rockets, bombs, propellers and rainbow orbs for huge magic blasts. Each win gives a star to place a nest, crystal tree, waterfall and more, and every finished area hatches a baby dragon that flies over your valley.

  • Hatch cute baby dragons from eggs on the board
  • Every finished area adds a new baby dragon friend
  • Magical match 3 with big combos and its own fantasy music
  • Earn coins and stars as you play
  • Made for Fire tablets, old and new models
  • Plays fully offline
