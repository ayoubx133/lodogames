About the game

Two copies of a cozy living room sit one above the other, but some things are different. A cushion changed color, a picture was swapped, a toy appeared on the rug or the sleepy cat turned around. Tap each change on either picture to circle it, and use a hint when you are stuck. Find them all with few misses to earn three stars and coins for fun marker styles.

  • Hand made changes: colors, flips, swaps and new items
  • New room views and more changes as you go
  • Hints, no timer and no fail, relaxing for all ages
  • Earn coins and stars as you play
  • Made for Fire tablets, old and new models
  • Plays fully offline
