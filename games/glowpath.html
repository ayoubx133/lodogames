About the game

Tap a tile to turn it and connect the glowing wires from the happy battery to every bulb. Wires split and branch, so each bulb needs its own path of light. When a bulb gets power it wakes up with a big smile and the little town below lights its windows. There is no timer, so take your time, and tap HINT when a tile needs turning.

  • Relaxing tap to turn wire puzzles with no timer
  • Branching paths and cute bulbs that wake up smiling
  • Collect glowing light colours with your coins
  • Earn coins and stars as you play
  • Made for Fire tablets, old and new models
  • Plays fully offline
