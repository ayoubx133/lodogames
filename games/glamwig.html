Brush, dye, curl and braid fabulous wigs for cute pet customers.

About the game

Cute pet customers bring an order card to your glam wig salon. Pick the right wig and drop it on the mannequin, brush the frizz away, paint dye strokes with your finger, then curl, shine or braid the hair. Finish the look with tiaras, bows, flowers and sparkly clips. Match every wish on the order to earn three stars and coins for new tool colors.

  • Brush, dye, curl and braid with your finger
  • Six wigs, six dye colors and sparkly accessories
  • Cute pet customers with their own orders
  • Earn coins and stars as you play
  • Made for Fire tablets, old and new models
  • Plays fully offline
