Sticker Diary Doll DIY

CasualFreeOfflineNo ads

Coming soon to the Amazon Appstore for Fire tablets

Peel stickers to dress your doll and decorate cute themed diary pages.

About the game

Every level is a new diary page with a theme like Party Day, Picnic or Fairy Garden. Peel outfit stickers off the sheet with your finger and stick them on the doll, then decorate the page with rainbows, balloons, kittens and more. Drag washi tape along the dotted lines to finish the page and get your stamp. Match the theme to earn three stars and unlock new hair colors for your doll.

  • Peel and stick real feeling stickers with your finger
  • Dress the sticker doll for six fun themed pages
  • Washi tape, page turns and calm acoustic music
  • Earn coins and stars as you play
  • Made for Fire tablets, old and new models
  • Plays fully offline
