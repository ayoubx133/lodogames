Match fresh crops, clean up spreading mud and build a happy farm.

About the game

Swap tomatoes, carrots, corn, cabbages, eggplants and apples to line up three or more. Mud puddles spread to a neighbouring tile if you leave them alone, so splash them with nearby matches, and clear straw patches, open wooden fences and bring egg baskets down to the bottom. Make rockets, bombs, propellers and rainbow orbs and combine them for big harvests, with five free moves whenever you are stuck. Every win earns a star to build a barn, chicken coop, windmill, duck pond and more.

  • Mud that spreads when ignored keeps every level lively
  • Juicy specials, combos and egg basket goals
  • Build a cozy farm one star at a time
  • Earn coins and stars as you play
  • Made for Fire tablets, old and new models
  • Plays fully offline
