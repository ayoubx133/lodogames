About the game

Swap pearls, rubies, sapphires, emeralds, gold coins and amethysts to line up three or more. Break barrels, clear sandy patches and bring treasure chests down to the bottom of the board by matching underneath them. Rockets, bombs, propellers and rainbow orbs clear the way, and two specials together make an even bigger blast. Every win earns a star to add a pirate ship, lighthouse, palm tree and more to your sunny cove.

  • Treasure chest levels where you guide chests to the bottom
  • Explosive specials and combos with friendly pirate fun
  • Build up a sunny pirate cove one star at a time
  • Earn coins and stars as you play
  • Made for Fire tablets, old and new models
  • Plays fully offline
