Coming soon to the Amazon Appstore for Fire tablets

Pop rainbow bubble boards in cute shapes, flip them and race your bubbly Pal.

About the game

Tap or swipe across a silicone board to pop every bubble with a soft, satisfying sound. Some boards flip over when you finish so you can pop the other side, some only want the glowing bubbles popped, and on race boards your friendly Pal pops the other half. Boards come in hearts, stars, kitties, flowers, fish and more. Pop fast and carefully to earn three stars.

  • Swipe to pop many bubbles at once
  • Flip, pattern and race boards
  • Six board color sets to collect
  • Earn coins and stars as you play
  • Made for Fire tablets, old and new models
  • Plays fully offline
